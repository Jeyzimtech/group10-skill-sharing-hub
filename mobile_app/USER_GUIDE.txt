=========================================
      SKILLS SHARING HUB - USER GUIDE
=========================================

Welcome to the Skills Sharing Hub, a premium platform designed to connect expert tutors with eager students. This guide provides an overview of how the application works, information about the development team, and how to get support.

-----------------------------------------
1. HOW THE APPLICATION WORKS
-----------------------------------------

The Skills Sharing Hub is designed to make knowledge exchange seamless and interactive. Here is the step-by-step flow:

A. GETTING STARTED
   - Account Creation: Sign up using your email and a secure password.
   - Profile Setup: Customize your profile to showcase your interests or expertise.

B. DISCOVERING SKILLS
   - Home Dashboard: Explore featured tutors and trending categories.
   - Search & Filter: Use the powerful search bar to find specific skills like Programming, Design, Music, or Business.
   - Skill Categories: Browse through curated categories to find the right niche.

C. CONNECTING WITH TUTORS
   - Tutor Profiles: View detailed profiles including bios, listed skills, and community ratings.
   - Smart Messaging: Use the built-in chat system to discuss learning goals and schedule sessions.
   - Real-Time Synchronization: Our system ensures you are always in the right conversation thread.

D. LEARNING SESSIONS
   - Video Calling: Engage in face-to-face learning via high-quality, low-latency video calls powered by Agora.
   - Interactive Tools: Use camera switching and microphone muting to manage your session effectively.

E. FUNDA MWANA AI TUTOR
   - 24/7 Assistance: Access the AI Study Buddy anytime for concept explanations and educational support.
   - Floating Interface: Tap the robot icon from any screen to get instant help.

F. BECOMING A TUTOR
   - Share Knowledge: If you have a skill to share, use the 'Become a Tutor' feature to list your expertise and start helping others.

-----------------------------------------
3. SUPPORT & FEEDBACK
-----------------------------------------

We are committed to providing a smooth experience. If you encounter any issues:

A. IN-APP SUPPORT
   - Report a Bug: Navigate to 'Settings' > 'Support & Feedback' to report technical issues directly.
   - Suggest Improvements: We value your feedback! Use the suggestion tool in the settings to help us improve.

B. TECHNICAL SUPPORT
   - Official Repository: https://github.com/Jeyzimtech/group10-skill-sharing-hub.git
   - Issues: Technical bugs can be tracked on our official GitHub repository.
   - Contact: For urgent inquiries, please reach out via the support channels listed in the application settings.
